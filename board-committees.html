Responsibilities

What each committee does

Architectural Review Board Chair: Carole Smith

The primary responsibility of the Architectural Review Board is to preserve the aesthetic qualities of the development and the quality of living in the community. The ARB establishes the standards with which all residents must comply, and assists in enforcing community standards within the association's architectural scheme as described in the community documents.

The ARB Chairman is appointed by the Board of Directors. The Chairman selects at least three members, and no more than five, to carry out the committee's responsibilities.

Landscape Committee Chair: Michelle Petrarca

The Landscape Committee monitors, researches, and recommends community-wide landscaping improvements, changes, and additions. It identifies needs, researches options, obtains estimates, and takes its findings to the Board of Directors for approval and funding.

The committee is currently taking active steps to facilitate conformance with Florida's state-wide rules concerning Florida Friendly Landscaping ordinances.

Pool Committee Chair: Judene Tippett

The Pool Committee monitors the pool, the pool house, and the pool grounds, and recommends improvements, changes, and additions as necessary. The committee identifies needs, researches options, oversees securing price estimates, and takes its findings to the Board of Directors for approval and funding.

Welcome Committee Chair: Linda Ritz

The Welcome Committee reaches out to new families on arrival and ensures they are made to feel welcome. The committee also introduces new arrivals to Florida, St. Johns County, and the City of St. Augustine government and business entities, and familiarizes them with the World Golf Village and Turnberry websites.

Enforcement Appeals Committee Chair: Carmen Gautile

The Enforcement Appeals Committee acts on behalf of association members to ensure that the Covenants and Restrictions — designed to enhance the beauty and value of property within Turnberry — are fairly applied to all residents. The committee's goal is to preserve the quality of living within the community.

Camera Awareness Committee Chair: Harry Tippett

The committee identifies safety and security concerns, develops solutions, and implements them once directed by the Board of Directors, concerning the health, safety, and security of Turnberry residents.

Social Committee Co-chairs: Marcy Carreras & Chris Worthington

The Social Committee advises and assists the Board in creating and providing social and recreational events geared to the interests and needs of the community. It also keeps homeowners informed about their association through the Turnberry website and the newsletter.

Holiday Committee Chair: Christopher Schraer

The Holiday Committee organizes seasonal decoration and celebration throughout the neighborhood, working alongside the Social Committee on community events.

Website Webmaster: Steve Cashat

The webmaster maintains the community website, publishing association documents, notices, and photography for residents.

Committee chairpersons are appointed by the Board of Directors. Each chair selects an adequate number of members to carry out the committee's responsibilities.
