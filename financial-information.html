How it works

Requesting a document

Send your request to the Treasurer. Requested information is returned by email. Your request must include all four of the following:

  • Your full name
  • Your Turnberry address
  • Your email address
  • The financial document requested
Request a document

Who handles requests

Financial document requests go to the association Treasurer. Choose “Financial document request” as the subject on the contact form and it will be routed correctly.

A note on voting rights

Under the By-Laws (V.4) and Florida Statutes 720.305(4), the association suspends the voting rights of any member more than 90 days in default on an assessment. The full text is on the covenants and restrictions page.

Open books, open meetings

Board meetings are open to every resident.
