Community rules · Section III

Pets in Turnberry

A maximum of three domestic household pets per home. Pets must be leashed off your property, and waste collected and disposed of properly.

  • Keep pets off neighbors' lawns and bushes
  • No pets at the pool, playground, or picnic areas
  • Owners are responsible for supervision and barking

Water conservation · Year-round

Lawn watering days

Irrigate only on your assigned day. Your day depends on whether your house number is even or odd.

  • Even house numbers water Sunday
  • Odd house numbers water Saturday
  • Restrictions protect our lakes and drinking supply

Safety & security · Ongoing

Habits that prevent break-ins

Most incidents in the neighborhood involve unlocked vehicles. A few minutes each night makes the difference.

  • Lock cars; remove keys, remotes, and valuables
  • Leave garage and porch lights on overnight
  • Keep car keys bedside — the panic button is an alarm
